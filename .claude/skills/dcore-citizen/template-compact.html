How to read this report

How this report works

We follow each question the debate took up (a thread) and sort every moment into one of three kinds:

  • Progress: something got settled. Everyone agreed on a point, or a question got a straight answer.
  • Left open: a point aimed at someone got no reply, or the answer left part of it open (a hollow dot).
  • Distraction: a move that pulled the debate off its question, like changing the subject or attacking motives.

Failed to reach is a step that was close: one or two moves away, using only what had already been said.

Accountability shows each voice's moves, one dot per moment, in the same three colours. We judge moves, never sides or opinions.

Each thread's replay is one fair way it could have gone, not what happened.
