~/smarchone/dcore

Debates, reviewed like a chess game.

tl;dr A chess engine doesn't tell you which side to play. It shows the better move you missed and what it cost. /dcore does the same for a debate, panel or townhall, in about a minute.

The problem
  • You watch an hour of debate and come away with the loudest slogan.
  • Questions go unanswered, and nobody notices the talk moved on.
  • Two sides agree on something and never say so. The chance is gone.
The fix
Progress

What was reached, and what slipped.

Agreements and sharper disagreements, plus the step one or two moves away that nobody took.

Distractions

Where the time went.

Progress, talk nobody replied to, and moves like "changed the subject", each with who and when.

Replay

One fair way it could have gone.

Same people, same views, every point answered. Grades moves, never positions.
