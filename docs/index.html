dcore

A review for public debates. In about a minute, see what a conversation actually achieved, what it missed, and why.

A chess engine doesn't tell you which side to play. It shows the better move you missed and what it cost. dcore does the same for a debate, panel or townhall.

  • What was reached: where speakers agreed, or sharpened their disagreement.
  • What was missed: progress one or two moves away, and the moment it slipped.
  • Where the time went: progress, talk nobody replied to, and distractions.
  • Who did what: every voice's good moves and misses, host included. No scores.
  • A fair replay: one way a stuck thread could have gone, same people, same views.

Example

Are Indian cities broken because Indians lack civic sense? 49-minute studio debate Reached: civic sense is learned; citizens should demand accountability. Missed: cheap bribes and cheap fines were the same point. Nobody noticed.
